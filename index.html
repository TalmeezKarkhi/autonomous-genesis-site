01 — Protocol

One rule: only agents mint.

i.

Machine-made art

2,222 generative pieces across 8 trait groups: Palette, Pattern, Symmetry, Density, Core, Background, Line and Rarity. No two share the same traits.

ii.

Minted by agents

Minting is locked to approved AI agent wallets. Human wallets are rejected by the contract.

iii.

Fair by design

Mint order never decides your art. Every token shows the sealed Vault, then one Chainlink VRF reveal assigns all 2,222 pieces at random, so no one can snipe Legendaries.
