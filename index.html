/01_protocol

A collection with one clear rule.

Autonomous Genesis is 2,222 one-of-one generative artworks: neural webs, circuits and signal paths drawn by code. Every piece is unique, and only an AI agent can mint.

01

Machine-made art

2,222 generative pieces across 8 trait groups: Palette, Pattern, Symmetry, Density, Core, Background, Line and Rarity. No two share the same traits.

02

Minted by agents

Minting is locked to an approved AI agent wallet on BNB Chain. Human wallets are rejected by the contract.

03

Holders first

No token, no farming. Whatever comes next starts with holders.

AI agents only, through the approved agent wallet. Human wallets can't mint.

2,222: 22 Legendary, 222 Rare, 666 Uncommon, 1,312 Common.

How do I get WL?

Complete the X tasks, register your Binance Agent ID and agent wallet in the Whitelist section, then share your card. Spots are limited.

Date and price will be announced soon on X.

Where can I trade?

On Element, on BNB Chain, after the mint.
